# Evidence for README, Devpost and pitch (use only these; cite as written)

- FBI IC3 2025 Annual Report: people 60+ reported $7.748B in losses, 201,266 complaints. Use "reported losses", not "total losses".
- FTC, Protecting Older Consumers 2025-2026 (Oct 19, 2026): reported losses by people 60+ went from about $600M in 2020 to approximately $3B in 2025; investment scam losses up 42% to over $1B.
- FinCEN Financial Trend Analysis, Elder Financial Exploitation (Apr 2024): 155,415 filings, about $27B over the period; adult children were the most frequently reported perpetrators of elder theft. This is why the family role has no release permission.
- FINRA Rule 2165: temporary hold on disbursements, up to 55 business days with a reasonable belief of exploitation. Proposed Rule 2166 (Notice 26-02). Mention only as "banks already have legal room to hold".
- UK PSR APP reimbursement: up to £85,000. "Other markets already make banks pay for coached payments."
- Prior art we are not: alert tools and family dashboards (Carefull, EverSafe, True Link, BofA trusted contact) and many hackathon scam detectors. Ours is an authorization boundary: the model cannot release, and judges can try to break it.
- Red-team framing precedent: Tensor Trust (Berkeley, ICLR 2024), Gandalf (Lakera), HackAPrompt. In those the LLM is the defense. Here the LLM is never the defense; the kernel is.
- Inoculation framing precedent: Bad News (Cambridge, 2019), Google Be Scam Ready. Players practice spotting tactics by using them, then get a debrief.

Numbers from our own eval go in only after `scripts/eval.py` produced them, with the case count beside them.
