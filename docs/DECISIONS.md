- 2026-10-24 00:58 (agent) Audit chain anchor is in-process only: `AuditLog` remembers the last (seq, hash) it wrote and refuses to append on a tail mismatch, and `verify` checks the chain plus the anchor. The SpacetimeDB `audit_mirror` table gives a second, out-of-process copy of the last 200 hashes for the board. Undo: none; a future version can sign the head with a key held outside the process.

Undo: move `rail.submit` to a worker and add a RELEASING state.
